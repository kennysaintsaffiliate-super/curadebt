Worth knowing

What tax relief can actually involve

The IRS does have formal programs for people who owe more than they can pay. An installment agreement spreads what you owe over time. An offer in compromise can, in some cases, settle a debt for less than the full amount — but the IRS only approves a minority of applications, based on a close look at your income, expenses, and assets, so it's not something every applicant qualifies for. Penalty abatement can sometimes reduce added fees, especially for a first-time issue. Which of these applies to you, if any, depends entirely on your specific numbers.

A specialist can also tell you when none of these is the right move, and a simpler payment plan directly with the IRS makes more sense.
