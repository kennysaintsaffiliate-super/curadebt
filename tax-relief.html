Frequently Asked Questions

What types of tax debt do you handle?

We handle IRS tax debt including unpaid taxes, penalties, interest, and levies. We also handle state tax debt where applicable.

What is an Offer in Compromise?

An Offer in Compromise is an IRS program that allows you to settle your tax debt for less than the full amount owed if you cannot pay in full.

How long does the tax resolution process take?

Timeline varies by resolution method: Offer in Compromise (6-12 months), Installment Agreement (ongoing payments), Currently Not Collectible (temporary suspension). Our specialists provide timelines during consultation.
