What types of debt do you handle?

We handle unsecured debts including credit cards, personal loans, medical bills, and business debt. Secured debts like mortgages and car loans are typically not included.

Will debt relief affect my credit score?

Enrolling in a debt relief program may initially impact your credit score, but many clients see improvement as they reduce and eliminate their debt. Our specialists can explain the details during your consultation.

Clients typically reduce their unsecured debt by 40-60% through negotiation. The exact amount depends on your total debt, creditors, and financial situation.
